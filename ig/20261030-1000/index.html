5 things a broom factory checks before choosing a bristle supplier

Before choosing a bristle supplier, a careful broom and brush factory usually has a checklist. Here are 5 things worth checking:

1. Fiber diameter consistency, for neat and durable brooms.
2. Stable nylon quality from batch to batch.
3. Bristle stiffness that suits the function of the final product.
4. Production capacity, so supply stays secure when orders grow.
5. Custom, OEM, and private label options, plus how responsive the team is on revisions.
