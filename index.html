About Us

Who We Are

PT Indofilament Global Plastic is an Indonesian manufacturer dedicated to producing high-quality nylon bristles for the brush and broom industry. We launched full operations in February 2026, equipped with the best and most advanced filament machinery available in Indonesia.

Our team combines deep technical expertise with a commitment to environmentally responsible manufacturing, using high-grade materials to deliver bristles that are strong, consistent, and durable.

From day one, we have built trusted partnerships — including with our current client Nagoya — and we are actively seeking to grow our network of brush and broom manufacturers across the region.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything brush and broom manufacturers usually ask before ordering bristle filament from Indonesia.

What bristle filaments does Indofilament manufacture?

We manufacture high-quality nylon bristles and bristle filaments for brooms and brushes: brush bristles, broom bristles and custom / OEM filament in many colors, including our Gold Series (Copper Gold and Bright Gold) pearl finish.

Do you supply international / export buyers?

Yes. Our capacity of up to 3 tons per day is built for both the Indonesian domestic market and export buyers. Contact us on WhatsApp (+62 831-6591-3330) or email for export quotations, packing and shipping terms.

Can I order custom colors, diameter, length or stiffness?

Yes. We accommodate specific colors, filament diameter, length and stiffness for OEM and private-label brush and broom manufacturers. Tell us your specification and application (broom, floor brush, toilet brush, dish brush, nail brush, hair brush, paint brush or industrial brush).

Can I get samples before placing an order?

Yes. Send your requirements through WhatsApp or the contact form, or visit our factory in Tangerang, Banten to see the production and collect samples.

Why choose Indofilament bristles?

Our high-grade nylon bristles give brooms and brushes strong, flexible and durable performance from Indonesia's best filament machinery, at pricing that competes with imported filament.

Where is your factory and who are your clients?

Our factory is at Jl. Perancis No.79, Dadap, Kosambi, Tangerang, Banten 15211, Indonesia, in the Greater Jakarta area. We supply brush and broom manufacturers, including Nagoya, one of Indonesia's leading broom, brush and mop brands.
