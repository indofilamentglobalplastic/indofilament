Indonesian-Made Broom Bristles: The Indofilament and Nagoya Partnership

Published 2026-10-24 · PT Indofilament Global Plastic

For many years, a large share of the filament used in household brooms and brushes came from abroad. Today that picture is starting to change. Since February 2026, PT Indofilament Global Plastic has supplied nylon bristles for the entire range of broom and brush products made by Nagoya, one of Indonesia's leading brands of brooms, brushes and mops. The result is a product chain that stays in Indonesia from filament to finished goods.

Why Indonesian-made broom bristles matter

Bristles are the part of a broom or brush that does the real work. They determine how well a broom sweeps, how a toilet brush reaches into corners, and how long a scrubbing brush keeps its shape. When the bristle supply depends on imports, manufacturers must deal with long lead times, shipping uncertainty and limited room to adjust specifications.

Bristles made in Indonesia change that. A manufacturer can discuss requirements directly with the filament producer, request samples, and even visit the factory. That closeness makes planning easier and speeds up product development.

About Nagoya

Nagoya is a well-known Indonesian brand of brooms, brushes and mops. The company manufactures its products in Indonesia and is widely recognised as one of the country's leading names in its category. You can learn more about the brand on its website, nagoyamopbroom.com, or on Instagram at @nagoyamopbroom.

We are proud to be part of its supply chain.

Since the start of operations, we have supplied nylon bristles for all of Nagoya's broom and brush products. The partnership shows that local filament can meet the needs of a major national brand.

From filament to finished product, all in Indonesia

The partnership is built on one clear idea: a complete production chain in the country.

  • Filament: nylon bristles produced by Indofilament in Tangerang.
  • Finished products: brooms, brushes and mops manufactured by Nagoya in Indonesia.

With both stages done domestically, value is created locally at every step, including jobs, technical skills and supporting industries. For buyers, it also means shorter supply lines between the filament factory and the product factory.

Supporting TKDN and reducing import dependence

The Indonesian government encourages manufacturers to raise their domestic component level, known as TKDN (Tingkat Komponen Dalam Negeri). Raw materials are an important part of that effort. When a key component such as bristle filament is sourced from a local factory instead of being imported, a manufacturer has a stronger basis for increasing the local content of its products.

Beyond TKDN, local supply also lowers dependence on imported materials. Manufacturers become less exposed to shipping delays and supply disruptions, and they can respond faster to changes in market demand. This is how the Indofilament and Nagoya partnership supports Indonesian industry in a practical way: not through slogans, but through everyday purchasing and production.

An option for other broom and brush manufacturers

If you run a broom or brush factory, are a distributor, or import filament for resale, the same local supply is available to you.
