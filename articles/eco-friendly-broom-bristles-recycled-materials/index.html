Eco-Friendly Broom Bristles: Recycled Raw Material, Reliable Nylon Filament

Published 2026-11-18 · PT Indofilament Global Plastic

For broom and brush manufacturers, sustainability is no longer just a nice story on the packaging. Retailers, distributors and end customers increasingly ask where a product comes from and what it is made of. This is why eco-friendly broom bristles have become a practical topic for factory owners and brand managers, not only for marketing teams.

At PT Indofilament Global Plastic in Tangerang, Banten, we produce broom bristles and brush filament from recycled plastic raw material. The result is a nylon filament that works like the bristles your production line already knows, with an environmental story you can honestly tell your buyers.

What Does Eco-Friendly Mean for Broom Bristles?

The word "eco-friendly" is often used loosely, so it helps to be specific. In our case, the raw material used to make the filament is recycled plastic. Plastic that has already been used is given a second life as a useful product instead of being left as waste. This also helps reduce reliance on virgin raw material in the broom and brush supply chain.

We describe the finished product simply as nylon filament, because that is what you receive and handle: a bristle for brooms and brushes. The recycled origin is about the raw material, not a change in how the bristle is used on your machine or in the customer's hand.

Why Brands and Factories Care

  • Stronger brand story. A broom or brush made from recycled raw material gives your sales team a clear point to explain to retailers and distributors.
  • Fit for modern buyers. Importers and retail chains increasingly prefer suppliers who can talk about responsible materials.
  • No need to change your process. The filament is supplied for the same tufting and assembly work you already do.
  • Room for differentiation. In a market full of similar-looking products, material origin can set a brand apart.

Bristles That Still Perform

An eco-friendly claim only matters if the product still does its job. Broom bristles must sweep cleanly, hold their shape and look good on the shelf. That is why our focus is on consistent filament quality, supported by the best and most advanced filament machines in Indonesia. Our plant has been fully operational since February 2026.

Diameter, length and stiffness can be adjusted to your needs, so the same recycled-material approach can be applied to a soft indoor broom, a firmer outdoor broom or a brush for tougher cleaning tasks.

Colors and Gold Series

Our standard production colors are yellow, green, blue and pink, plus the Gold Series. The Gold Series comes in two variants, Bright Gold and Copper Gold, both with a pearl finish that gives the bristles a premium look. If your line needs red, purple, orange, black, white or a custom color, those can be ordered as well.

This matters for brands that want an eco-friendly product without a dull appearance. Color and finishing are part of how a broom sells, and recycled raw material does not limit your design options.
