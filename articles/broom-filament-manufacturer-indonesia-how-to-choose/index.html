Broom Filament Manufacturers in Indonesia: How to Choose the Right Company for Broom and Brush Bristles

Published 2026-10-25 · PT Indofilament Global Plastic

If you are searching for a broom manufacturer in Indonesia, the first thing to clarify is what you actually need to buy. Some companies make finished brooms, while others make the bristle material (filament) that goes into those brooms and brushes. This guide explains the difference, the criteria for choosing a good bristle supplier, and where PT Indofilament Global Plastic fits in.

Two Kinds of Companies, Two Different Products

In Indonesia, the phrase "broom company" can mean two very different businesses.

1. Broom and brush makers (finished products)

These companies assemble complete products: brooms, floor brushes, toilet brushes, dish brushes and so on. They buy bristles, handles, and other components, then assemble and sell the finished item to retailers or consumers.

2. Bristle manufacturers (raw material)

These companies produce the filament, the bristles themselves. They do not sell a finished broom. They sell bristle material to broom and brush factories, distributors, and importers. This is where the quality of the end product begins, because the filament determines how a broom sweeps, how a brush scrubs, and how long it lasts.

If you run a broom or brush factory, or you distribute or import bristle material, you are looking for the second type: a bulu sapu and bulu sikat manufacturer.

Criteria for Choosing a Broom and Brush Bristle Manufacturer

Production capacity and consistency

Your production line cannot stop because bristle supply runs short. Ask how much the factory can produce per day, and whether it can supply regularly, not just once. Consistent color, thickness, and stiffness from one batch to the next matter just as much as capacity, because uneven bristles lead to uneven finished products.

Modern machinery

Filament quality depends heavily on the machines. Modern extrusion and processing equipment produces more uniform diameters and a cleaner finish. When evaluating a supplier, ask about the machinery they use and, if possible, see it in person.

Ability to customize

Every product has its own needs. A floor broom, a toilet brush, a nail brush, and a paint brush all call for different bristles. A good manufacturer can adjust diameter, length, and stiffness, and can provide the colors you need, including custom colors.

Range of products and colors

A supplier with a broad range lets you source several bristle types from one place, which simplifies purchasing and quality control. Check whether the factory supplies bristles for the applications you make: brooms, floor brushes, toilet brushes, dish brushes, washing brushes, washing machine brushes, nail brushes, hair brushes, paint brushes, and industrial brushes.

OEM and private label support

If you are building your own brand, look for a manufacturer that accepts custom filament and private label orders, so the bristle specification can follow your product design.

Samples and transparency

A reliable manufacturer is willing to send samples so you can test the bristles on your own production line before ordering. It should also welcome factory visits. Both are signs of a supplier confident in its quality.

Export and domestic experience

If you are an importer or you sell across markets, check that the supplier can serve both domestic and export customers, and that communication is clear and responsive.

Environmental responsibility

More buyers now ask about sustainability. A manufacturer that uses recycled plastic as its raw material can help you offer products that are friendlier to the environment, which is a useful selling point for your own brand.
